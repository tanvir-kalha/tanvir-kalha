## About

I design digital products for B2B SaaS and AI driven platforms, three years in, with a focus on turning complicated workflows into something a user barely notices using. Most of my work sits at the intersection of research, systems thinking, and interface craft.

Outside of client work, I write about design and AI, and spend a fair amount of time experimenting with AI powered storytelling.
